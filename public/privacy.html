Paper Plane Run · Player support

Privacy Policy

This policy covers Paper Plane Run on iPhone, iPad, and the web. The game does not require an account, show ads, or track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

On your device

Your callsign, settings, flights, unlocks, device scores, and diagnostic history are stored in the app or browser. You can export progress in Settings. A backup includes your callsign and progress. When you share a backup, route, challenge, or image, you choose where it goes. The game does not upload those files for you.

Online scores

Opening an online board requests rankings from the game’s servers. On iPhone and iPad, you can turn on Publish anonymous scores in Settings. Eligible flights then send distance, stars, difficulty, and route context with a short-lived run ticket. The public board lists those scores as “Pilot.” Turning publishing off stops new submissions. On the web, eligible scores are submitted automatically with the callsign you enter. Use a nickname you are comfortable making public. Online rankings are public and can outlast the game data saved on your device.

Service and diagnostics

Vercel hosts the game and score endpoints. Online requests expose network information such as your IP address, which can be used to limit abusive traffic. The website sends gameplay events with a temporary session identifier for aggregate diagnostics. The iOS app keeps its gameplay diagnostics on the device.

Privacy requests

To ask about your data or request removal of a public score, email Paper Plane Run support. Include the approximate date, mode, distance, and star count so we can locate the score.

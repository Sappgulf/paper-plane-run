Paper Plane Run · Privacy policy

Player Support

Learn to fly

Open How to Fly for controls and a practice flight. In Settings you can switch between Touch Aim and Joystick, enlarge the controls, or turn on reduced motion.

Save your progress

Open Settings and choose Export progress before moving to a new device or reinstalling. Save or copy the file somewhere you can find it, then use Restore a saved flight log on the destination device.

Gameplay and scores

Journey, practice, custom routes, and regular flights work offline. The Online, Daily, and Weekly boards need a connection. Start with the Device board if online scores are unavailable.

Contact support

Email Paper Plane Run support for help. Include your device, game version, flight mode, and what happened. Keep progress backups and other private files out of your first message.
